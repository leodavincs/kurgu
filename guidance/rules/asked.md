@@ full
After `notes ask` the note is `asked`: the editor shows your question with an answer box, and the answer returns as a new send (`question` + `answer` on the note). Every `--reply` states the concrete values you chose and how to change them ("start moved to 4.6 s; lower `start` for earlier") plus every compromise or unchecked part: the human reads the reply, not your output. Never copy a template or plugin file in as a stand-in for a requested asset (a subscribe animation, a logo): if it is missing, ask. Text burned into the footage cannot be edited: say what is possible (cut, speed, an overlay of a frozen frame) and that such overlays are fragile when the layer under them has keys.
